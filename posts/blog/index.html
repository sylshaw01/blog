I have wanted to make a blog for years and years, so… thought I should at least post something and try :)

For a long time I feel a hesitancy to public blog-posting. I hold a fear that if people were to see the things that I struggle on, they would realise how bad I am at whatever I happen to be doing. I definitely used to care less about what people thought of me, and so perhaps this is an exercise in shedding some imposter-syndrome type feelings that have been building up.

I hope to be posting miscellaneous things about maths, physics or computer science, or just what I’m thinking about.
